WHY DO SMALL CIRCLES DISAPPEAR?

Circle area is πr². With a linear radius scale, area grows with count², exaggerating large values. A square-root radius scale makes area proportional to count.

Uses all mapped locations, including single-record places. Both scales use the same data, map view, domain, and 24 px maximum radius. No minimum radius is applied.
